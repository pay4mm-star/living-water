A clean property builds trust before a customer walks in. Living Water serves small businesses and large commercial organizations across Ontario, and many of our clients are property managers, condominium boards and facility managers who need dependable, insured contractors.

We specialise in removing stains, gum, graffiti and hazardous grease build-up, and in restoring brick and concrete. Work can be scheduled overnight or on weekends to avoid disrupting tenants and customers.

Benefits of commercial power washing

  • Improved appearance — clean facades, walkways and entrances help attract customers and protect your reputation.
  • Enhanced safety — removing grime, algae and grease helps prevent slips and falls.
  • Longer surface life — regular cleaning slows deterioration and avoids costly repairs and replacement.
