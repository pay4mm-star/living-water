Industrial cleaning demands more than horsepower. It needs a plan that protects your people, your equipment and your production schedule. The Living Water team has extensive experience with the unique requirements of industrial cleaning and remediation, and health and safety come first on every site.

Keeping equipment and machinery clean keeps it running smoothly and extends its working life. We work with you to build a cleaning schedule that is safe for your business, your employees and your workplace.

Our industrial services

  • Silo and tank cleaning, exterior and interior
  • Industrial plant pressure washing and sanitation
  • Food plant sanitation and disinfection
  • Manufacturing facility cleaning
  • Hydro jetting of condenser pipes and lines
  • Heavy equipment and crane degreasing
  • Bridges and dams — sandblasting or pressure washing
  • Mould remediation on equipment and facilities
  • Professional paint and coating removal

Certified for work at height

Our technicians are certified boom and scissor lift operators, so tall tanks, silos and facades can be cleaned without scaffolding delays.

Industrial Power Washing FAQ

Anything not covered here? Call (416) 663-8000 and ask.

What is hydro jetting?

Hydro jetting uses very high-pressure water streams to scour build-up from the inside of pipes, condensers and lines without abrasive media.

Do you offer emergency industrial cleaning?

Yes. Industrial and commercial clients can reach our 24/7 emergency line at (647) 833-4343.

Can you clean food processing plants?

Yes. We provide pressure washing, sanitation and disinfection for food plants, scheduled around production.
