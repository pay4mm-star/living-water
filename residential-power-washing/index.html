Residential Power Washing in Toronto & the GTA

Your home's exterior, gently restored. Driveways, interlock, siding, decks and walkways washed back to the colour you bought.

Get a Free QuoteCall (416) 663-8000

Living Water cleans the outside of homes across Toronto and the GTA — driveways, interlock, patios, brick, stone, stucco, siding, roofs, decks and fences — using low-pressure soft washing with biodegradable cleaners where high pressure would damage siding, mortar or window seals.

Ontario weather is hard on a house. Road salt, spring pollen, summer algae and autumn leaves leave driveways stained, interlock green and brick dull. Residential power washing removes that build-up safely, restoring curb appeal and protecting the materials you paid for.

High pressure is not always the answer. It can strip sand from interlock joints, etch soft stone, blow out mortar and force water behind siding. Our technicians choose between soft washing, hot-water pressure washing and gentle media cleaning for each surface — the judgement that comes from washing 7,500+ properties since 1998.

  • Driveways, interlock and pavers
  • Patios, pool decks and coping
  • Exterior and interior brick

  • Roofs: cedar, ceramic and metal
  • Gutters, siding and windows
  • Decks and fences
  • Efflorescence on walls, steps and stone
  • Sealant removal from pavers
  • Professional paint removal
  • Sandblasting for concrete and pools

Soft washing vs. pressure washing

Soft washing uses low pressure and biodegradable cleaners to dissolve algae, mildew and grime on siding, stucco and roofs. Pressure washing — often with hot water — lifts oil, tire marks and deep dirt from concrete and interlock. Most homes need both, and we tell you which surface gets which.

Sealing after cleaning

Once interlock, natural stone or pool coping is clean and dry, sealing can help it resist stains and fading. We will tell you honestly whether your surface benefits from a sealer.

Questions

Residential Power Washing FAQ

Anything not covered here? Call (416) 663-8000 and ask.

How often should I pressure wash my driveway?

Most GTA homeowners wash driveways and interlock every one to two years, ideally in spring to remove winter salt and again if moss or oil stains appear.

Can you clean my roof without damaging shingles?

Yes. Roofs are soft washed at low pressure with cleaners that kill moss and algae, so granules and shingles are not damaged. We clean cedar, ceramic and metal roofs.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not usually. We need access to an outdoor water tap and the areas being cleaned. We send before and after photos when the job is complete.

Do you move patio furniture?

Yes. Our crews move and replace patio furniture and protect plants, windows and nearby surfaces as part of the job.
