Why professional power washing?

Professional power washing removes dirt, algae, grease and stains while protecting the surface underneath — because the right equipment, pressure, water temperature and cleaning chemistry differ for every material.

  • Restores curb appeal and a fresh, clean appearance
  • Removes dirt, grime, algae, grease, stains and stubborn build-up
  • Helps protect exterior surfaces from premature wear
  • Improves safety by removing slippery contaminants
  • Uses professional equipment and surface-specific techniques
  • Saves time with efficient, consistent, reliable results

Frequently Asked Questions

Anything not covered here? Call (416) 663-8000 and ask.

How quickly can Living Water start?

Residential jobs are usually booked within 2–5 days, and same-week service is standard. Commercial and industrial clients get 24/7 emergency response on (647) 833-4343.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Living Water is fully licensed in Ontario, carries $5 million in commercial general liability insurance, is WSIB covered and ISSA-GBAC certified. We send a Certificate of Insurance to your property manager, condo board or HOA on request.

Will pressure washing damage my driveway, brick or siding?

Not when it is done right. We match the pressure, nozzle, water temperature and chemistry to each surface — interlock, brick, vinyl, stucco, stone and wood all need different approaches. Where high pressure is wrong we soft wash, steam or use media blasting instead.

Do you do commercial and industrial work?

Yes — refineries, plants, silos and tanks, heritage restoration, graffiti removal, sandblasting and elevator pit cleaning. Many of our clients are property managers, general contractors and municipalities. Our crews are certified boom and scissor lift operators.

What areas do you serve?

Toronto and the entire Greater Toronto Area, including Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Oakville, Burlington, Pickering, Ajax, Whitby and Oshawa, plus Hamilton, Niagara, Guelph, Cambridge, Barrie and Simcoe County.

Is the quote free?

Yes. Assessments are free and no-obligation, and you receive a fixed written quote before any work starts.
