Services

Fire, Water & Wind Damage Restoration

24/7 emergency response to secure, clean and restore your home or business after fire, flood, storm or mould.

Get a Free QuoteCall (416) 663-8000

Living Water provides 24/7 disaster restoration across Toronto and the GTA — fire and smoke damage cleaning, water extraction and drying, wind and storm damage securing and repair, and mould remediation — restoring homes and businesses to their pre-loss condition. Emergency line: (647) 833-4343.

When disaster strikes, every hour counts. Our team moves quickly to assess the damage, secure the property and start recovery — from a small kitchen fire to major storm damage.

Fire damage restoration

  1. Inspection and evaluation — a full walkthrough to assess structure, electrical and smoke- or soot-affected zones.
  2. Securing the property — boarding windows and entryways to stop weather exposure and unauthorized access.
  3. Smoke and soot remediation — removing particles that degrade air quality and keep damaging surfaces.
  4. Rebuilding and restoration — structural repairs, drywall and finishing once the property is safe.

Water damage restoration

Floods, leaks, burst pipes and failed appliances escalate quickly into mould and structural damage. We find and stop the source, extract standing water with industrial pumps and vacuums, dry walls, floors and ceilings with commercial dehumidifiers, then repair drywall, flooring and structural elements.

Wind & storm damage restoration

We inspect for torn roofing, broken windows, stripped siding and hidden structural damage; tarp roofs and board openings to stop water getting in; complete permanent repairs; and recommend upgrades that make the property more resilient to the next storm.

Mould removal & remediation

Our technicians locate visible and hidden mould and the moisture behind it, contain the area with negative air pressure and barriers, remove affected materials, treat surfaces with EPA-approved antimicrobials, and restore the space.

Disaster Restoration FAQ

Anything not covered here? Call (416) 663-8000 and ask.

Do you respond to emergencies at night or on weekends?

Yes. Our emergency line, (647) 833-4343, is answered 24/7 for fire, water, wind and commercial emergencies.

Do you work with insurance claims?

We document damage with photos and written scopes that you can provide to your insurer. Contact your insurance company as soon as possible after a loss.

How fast must water damage be dried?

As soon as possible. Mould can begin growing within 24–48 hours, which is why extraction and drying start immediately.
