Pressure Washing & Restoration Glossary

Plain-English definitions of the terms used in pressure washing, blasting, masonry restoration and elevator pit cleaning.

Abrasive blasting
Driving a stream of abrasive media (sand, glass, garnet, soda, walnut shell and others) at a surface under pressure to strip rust, paint, scale or coatings. Learn more →
Acid wash
Cleaning masonry with a diluted acid-based cleaner to dissolve mortar smears and mineral stains. It must be tested, correctly diluted and neutralised. Learn more →
Anti-graffiti coating
A clear protective coating that makes graffiti easier to remove. Sacrificial coatings come off with the graffiti; permanent coatings stay in place. Learn more →
Buffer (elevator)
The spring or oil device at the bottom of an elevator pit that stops a car or counterweight travelling past its normal limit. Pit debris and corrosion around buffers is a common clean-up item. Learn more →
Dry ice blasting
Blasting with frozen carbon dioxide pellets that turn to gas on impact, removing coatings and residue without leaving abrasive media behind. Learn more →
Dustless blasting
Wet abrasive blasting where water is mixed with the media to suppress airborne dust. Learn more →
Efflorescence
A white, powdery salt deposit left on brick, concrete, stone or pavers when water moves through the material and evaporates at the surface. Learn more →
Elevator pit
The space at the bottom of an elevator hoistway, below the lowest landing, housing buffers and other equipment. It collects debris, water and oil. Learn more →
Hot-water pressure washing
Pressure washing with heated water, often called power washing, which cuts through oil, grease and gum faster than cold water. Learn more →
Hydro jetting
Using very high-pressure water streams to scour build-up from inside pipes, condensers and lines. Learn more →
Lime run
Hard white calcium carbonate deposits that streak from mortar joints, harder to remove than ordinary efflorescence. Learn more →
Micro blasting
Precision blasting with very fine media at low pressure, used on detailed or delicate masonry and stubborn coatings. Learn more →
Poultice
A paste applied to stone or brick that draws stains or paint ghosting out of the pores as it dries. Learn more →
Polymeric sand
Joint sand for interlock pavers mixed with binders that harden when misted with water, resisting washout and weeds. Learn more →
Pressure washing
Cleaning with high-pressure water to remove dirt, grime and stains from durable surfaces like concrete and interlock. Learn more →
Soda blasting
Blasting with sodium bicarbonate, a soft media suited to delicate surfaces, soot and light coatings. Learn more →
Soft washing
Low-pressure cleaning that uses biodegradable cleaners to kill and remove algae, mould and mildew from siding, stucco and roofs. Learn more →
Spalling
Flaking or popping of the surface of brick, stone or concrete, often caused by trapped moisture freezing and thawing. Learn more →
Sump pump
A pump in a pit or basin that removes collected water. In elevator pits, sumps must be covered and working to prevent flooding. Learn more →
Surface cleaner
A rotary attachment for pressure washers that cleans flat surfaces evenly without the stripes a bare wand leaves. Learn more →
WSIB clearance certificate
A document from Ontario's Workplace Safety and Insurance Board confirming a contractor's account is in good standing. Learn more →
