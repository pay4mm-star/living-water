Brick collects years of dirt, staining, paint, efflorescence and other build-up. Removing it is not simply a matter of more pressure — the wrong method can scar brick faces, wash out mortar, etch glass and kill landscaping. Once that damage is done, there is no going back.

That is why we assess the brick and the material to be removed before selecting a cleaning, chemical or blasting method — and always neutralise acid washes properly.

Brick Cleaning & Paint Removal FAQ

Anything not covered here? Call (416) 663-8000 and ask.

Can paint be removed from brick without damaging it?

In most cases, yes. We use chemical strippers and controlled low-pressure rinsing, or gentle media blasting for stubborn coatings, after testing a small area.

What is a brick acid wash?

An acid wash dissolves mortar smears and mineral stains from new or old brick. It must be correctly diluted, tested and neutralised to avoid burning the brick.

Do you clean interior brick?

Yes — interior brick walls and fireplaces can be cleaned with low-moisture methods and full protection of the room.
