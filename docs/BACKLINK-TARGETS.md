## Tier 1: highest value, closest to our best customers
| Target | Why it fits | How to get listed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

| Heritage contractor directories (Markham, Oakville, Caledon and other municipalities) | Municipal heritage staff publish lists of contractors and tradespeople who work on heritage buildings, including paint removal and masonry. Also see the CAHP directory (caphc.ca). | markhammuseum.ca Heritage Contractors Master Directory PDF; oakville.ca heritage specialist guide; caledon.ca Guide to Heritage Specialists (July 2026) |
| BOMA Toronto | Commercial building owners and managers. Has a membership directory and allied/affiliate classes for service providers. | Ask about Allied/Affiliate membership and cost. | issuu.com/bomatoronto (membership directory); bomatoronto.org |
