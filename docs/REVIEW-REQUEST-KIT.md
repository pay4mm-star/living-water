# Living Water – Google Review Request Kit

**Why this matters:** reviews are the biggest remaining gap versus competitors (interlock and pressure washing specialists often have 100+ reviews; we have 39 on Google, rated 4.9). More recent, detailed reviews lift Maps ranking, click-through and AI-assistant recommendations.

**Your review link (works anywhere):** https://g.page/r/CQaollgymrYtEBM/review
**Short link (after deploy):** https://livingwater-pws.com/review

## Rules (keeps the profile safe)
- Ask every customer, not just happy ones. Never offer money, discounts or gifts for a review (against Google policy and can get reviews removed).
- Do not ask for only positive reviews or pre-screen who gets the link.
- Never write reviews for customers.

## When to ask
Same day or within 48 hours of finishing a job, when the customer is looking at the result. Add the link to the invoice or the "job complete" message.

## What makes a review useful (suggest, don't script)
Mentioning the service (e.g. "interlock sealing", "elevator pit cleaning"), the city or neighbourhood, and what changed. Detailed reviews with these terms help local search.

## Replying to reviews (do within 48 hours)
- Thank them by name and mention the service and area, e.g. "Thanks, [Name]. Glad the interlock in Richmond Hill looks great after the wash and seal."
- For a negative review: apologize, stay factual, offer to fix it offline, never argue publicly.

## Targets
- Short term: 10 new Google reviews in 60 days. Then 3–5 a month ongoing.
- Add HomeStars and Houzz profiles and send the same ask for those.

## Optional: printed QR card
Google provides a QR code for the review link (Business Profile > Ask for reviews). Print it on invoices, truck cards or a leave-behind card.
