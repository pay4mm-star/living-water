Sandblasting & Dustless Media Blasting in Toronto

The efficient way to strip rust, scale, stains and old coatings and prepare surfaces for painting.

Get a Free QuoteCall (416) 663-8000

Living Water offers mobile dustless sandblasting, wet and dry abrasive blasting, micro blasting, soda blasting and dry ice blasting across Toronto and the GTA to remove rust, scale, paint and epoxy from steel, aluminium, brick, concrete and pools.

Sandblasting — also called abrasive blasting — drives a high-pressure stream of media against a surface to remove rust, scale, stains and old paint. It is the fastest way to prepare metal, masonry and concrete for painting, coating or other treatments.

We work for general contractors, pool painting companies, fire restoration firms, renovation and maintenance companies, and residential, commercial and industrial property owners. Our team can explain why a particular blasting method will — or will not — suit your project.

Why choose dustless blasting

  • Efficient and effective — removes rust, scale, stains and old coatings quickly, leaving a clean profile ready for treatment.
  • Versatile — steel and aluminium, brick facades, concrete pool shells, machinery, bridges, tanks, and water and sewage plants.
  • Environmentally responsible — dustless technology keeps airborne dust down and uses environmentally friendly media.

Questions

Sandblasting & Media Blasting FAQ

Anything not covered here? Call (416) 663-8000 and ask.

Is sandblasting safe for brick?

It can be, with the right media and pressure. Soft or heritage brick often needs micro blasting or soda blasting instead of sand. We assess first and test a small area.

What is dustless sandblasting?

Dustless blasting mixes water with the abrasive media so dust is captured at the surface, making it cleaner and safer for occupied or urban sites.

Do you blast swimming pools?

Yes. We blast concrete pool shells to remove failing paint so they can be repainted.
