Elevator Pit Cleaning in Toronto & the GTA

Oil spills, sludge, flooding and sewage backups cleaned from elevator pits — trusted by Ontario's leading elevator service providers.

Get a Free QuoteCall (416) 663-8000

Living Water cleans and sanitizes elevator pits across Toronto and the GTA — pressure washing, sludge and oil spill removal, hazardous waste disposal, crack repair, painting, deodorizing and sewage backup clean-up — and is a trusted referral for major Ontario elevator service companies.

Elevator pits are easy to forget, but garbage, dirt, oil and standing water build up over time. A dirty pit is a fire hazard, an environmental hazard and a problem for maintenance crews — and must be cleaned regularly.

Signs your pit needs cleaning: leaks, cracks, foul odours, accumulated garbage, or standing water and mud. Some of Ontario's leading elevator service providers count on us for their most challenging projects, including oil spills and flood clean-ups.

Elevator Pit Cleaning FAQ

Anything not covered here? Call (416) 663-8000 and ask.

How often should elevator pits be cleaned?

Pits should be inspected during routine elevator maintenance and cleaned whenever debris, oil or water is present — and immediately after any flood or sewage backup.

Do you dispose of oil and hazardous waste?

Yes. Oil, sludge and contaminated material are removed and disposed of properly.

Do you work with elevator contractors?

Yes. Elevator service companies regularly refer and subcontract pit cleaning to Living Water.
