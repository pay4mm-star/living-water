Specialised Cleaning

Metal Restoration, Rust Removal & Surface Preparation

Stainless steel, copper and aluminium cleaned, de-rusted and prepared so they look new again.

Get a Free QuoteCall (416) 663-8000

Living Water restores metal surfaces across Toronto and the GTA — removing rust, oxidation, old paint and coatings from steel, stainless steel, aluminium and copper — using cleaning, sandblasting or media blasting chosen for the metal and the finish required.

Stainless steel, copper and aluminium are durable, but all need maintenance to keep looking new. Rust, oxidation, old paint and grime affect both appearance and condition.

For some projects, blasting or another specialised method is better than standard pressure washing. With sandblasting and media blasting in-house, we select the approach based on the surface and the result you want.

Metal restoration services

  • Rust removal
  • Oxidation removal
  • Metal surface cleaning
  • Old coating removal
  • Paint removal
  • Surface preparation for coating
  • Copper and aluminium restoration
  • Sandblasting where appropriate

Can you remove rust without damaging the metal?

Yes. We select the blasting media and pressure to suit the metal so the substrate is cleaned without unnecessary profile or thinning.

Do you restore architectural copper and aluminium?

Yes — including heritage copper and aluminium elements on historic buildings.
