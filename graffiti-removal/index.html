Graffiti Removal Without Damaging the Surface

Same-week tag removal from brick, concrete, stone, metal, glass and painted surfaces — plus anti-graffiti coatings.

Get a Free QuoteCall (416) 663-8000

Living Water removes graffiti from brick, concrete, stone, metal and glass across Toronto and the GTA — usually within the same week — using bio-friendly chemical removers and controlled pressure washing selected for the surface, and can apply anti-graffiti coatings afterwards.

Graffiti changes how people see your building — and removing it is not just a matter of pressure. The wrong method leaves ghosting or damages brick, stone and concrete. We look at the surface, the type of paint or marker and the condition of the material before choosing a method.

Questions

Graffiti Removal FAQ

Anything not covered here? Call (416) 663-8000 and ask.

How fast can graffiti be removed?

We typically remove graffiti within the same week, and sooner for offensive tags or commercial clients.

Will the wall show a shadow afterwards?

Fresh graffiti removed with the right chemistry usually leaves no trace. Older tags on porous brick can leave light ghosting, which we minimise with poultices or gentle blasting.

What is an anti-graffiti coating?

A clear protective coating that makes future graffiti easier to remove without damaging the surface underneath.
