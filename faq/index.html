General

How quickly can Living Water start?

Residential jobs are usually booked within 2–5 days, and same-week service is standard. Commercial and industrial clients get 24/7 emergency response on (647) 833-4343.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Living Water is fully licensed in Ontario, carries $5 million in commercial general liability insurance, is WSIB covered and ISSA-GBAC certified. We send a Certificate of Insurance to your property manager, condo board or HOA on request.

Will pressure washing damage my driveway, brick or siding?

Not when it is done right. We match the pressure, nozzle, water temperature and chemistry to each surface — interlock, brick, vinyl, stucco, stone and wood all need different approaches. Where high pressure is wrong we soft wash, steam or use media blasting instead.

Do you do commercial and industrial work?

Yes — refineries, plants, silos and tanks, heritage restoration, graffiti removal, sandblasting and elevator pit cleaning. Many of our clients are property managers, general contractors and municipalities. Our crews are certified boom and scissor lift operators.

What areas do you serve?

Toronto and the entire Greater Toronto Area, including Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Oakville, Burlington, Pickering, Ajax, Whitby and Oshawa, plus Hamilton, Niagara, Guelph, Cambridge, Barrie and Simcoe County.

Is the quote free?

Yes. Assessments are free and no-obligation, and you receive a fixed written quote before any work starts.

Residential Power Washing

How often should I pressure wash my driveway?

Most GTA homeowners wash driveways and interlock every one to two years, ideally in spring to remove winter salt and again if moss or oil stains appear.

Can you clean my roof without damaging shingles?

Yes. Roofs are soft washed at low pressure with cleaners that kill moss and algae, so granules and shingles are not damaged. We clean cedar, ceramic and metal roofs.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not usually. We need access to an outdoor water tap and the areas being cleaned. We send before and after photos when the job is complete.

Do you move patio furniture?

Yes. Our crews move and replace patio furniture and protect plants, windows and nearby surfaces as part of the job.

Commercial Power Washing

Can you work after hours?

Yes. We regularly schedule commercial work overnight and on weekends so tenants, shoppers and staff are not disrupted.

Do you provide a Certificate of Insurance?

Yes. We carry $5 million commercial general liability and full WSIB coverage, and send a COI and WSIB clearance with every quote.

Can you set up a maintenance schedule?

Yes. Many property managers book seasonal or monthly cleaning for entrances, garages and walkways.

Industrial Power Washing

What is hydro jetting?

Hydro jetting uses very high-pressure water streams to scour build-up from the inside of pipes, condensers and lines without abrasive media.

Do you offer emergency industrial cleaning?

Yes. Industrial and commercial clients can reach our 24/7 emergency line at (647) 833-4343.

Can you clean food processing plants?

Yes. We provide pressure washing, sanitation and disinfection for food plants, scheduled around production.

Sandblasting & Media Blasting

Is sandblasting safe for brick?

It can be, with the right media and pressure. Soft or heritage brick often needs micro blasting or soda blasting instead of sand. We assess first and test a small area.

What is dustless sandblasting?

Dustless blasting mixes water with the abrasive media so dust is captured at the surface, making it cleaner and safer for occupied or urban sites.

Do you blast swimming pools?

Yes. We blast concrete pool shells to remove failing paint so they can be repainted.

Brick Cleaning & Paint Removal

Can paint be removed from brick without damaging it?

In most cases, yes. We use chemical strippers and controlled low-pressure rinsing, or gentle media blasting for stubborn coatings, after testing a small area.

What is a brick acid wash?

An acid wash dissolves mortar smears and mineral stains from new or old brick. It must be correctly diluted, tested and neutralised to avoid burning the brick.

Do you clean interior brick?

Yes — interior brick walls and fireplaces can be cleaned with low-moisture methods and full protection of the room.

Historical Remediation

Which cleaning methods are safe for heritage masonry?

Usually the gentlest effective method: low-pressure steam, poultices, soda or micro blasting at low pressure, and carefully tested chemical cleaners. High-pressure water and coarse sand are avoided on soft historic brick and stone.

Can you remove lead paint?

Yes. We remove lead-based paint from historic surfaces with containment and safe handling and disposal of waste.

Do you work with heritage consultants?

Yes. We regularly work alongside architects, heritage conservators and general contractors and can complete test panels for approval.

Disaster Restoration

Do you respond to emergencies at night or on weekends?

Yes. Our emergency line, (647) 833-4343, is answered 24/7 for fire, water, wind and commercial emergencies.

Do you work with insurance claims?

We document damage with photos and written scopes that you can provide to your insurer. Contact your insurance company as soon as possible after a loss.

How fast must water damage be dried?

As soon as possible. Mould can begin growing within 24–48 hours, which is why extraction and drying start immediately.

Elevator Pit Cleaning

How often should elevator pits be cleaned?

Pits should be inspected during routine elevator maintenance and cleaned whenever debris, oil or water is present — and immediately after any flood or sewage backup.

Do you dispose of oil and hazardous waste?

Yes. Oil, sludge and contaminated material are removed and disposed of properly.

Do you work with elevator contractors?

Yes. Elevator service companies regularly refer and subcontract pit cleaning to Living Water.

Efflorescence Removal

What causes efflorescence?

Water dissolves salts inside brick, mortar, concrete or stone, carries them to the surface and evaporates, leaving a white crystalline deposit.

Can pressure washing alone remove efflorescence?

Light, fresh efflorescence sometimes brushes or rinses off, but established deposits usually need a suitable masonry cleaner followed by controlled rinsing.

Is efflorescence covered by my stone warranty?

Many Ontario stone and paver manufacturers use Living Water as their warranty service provider. Check with your supplier or contact us.

Graffiti Removal

How fast can graffiti be removed?

We typically remove graffiti within the same week, and sooner for offensive tags or commercial clients.

Will the wall show a shadow afterwards?

Fresh graffiti removed with the right chemistry usually leaves no trace. Older tags on porous brick can leave light ghosting, which we minimise with poultices or gentle blasting.

What is an anti-graffiti coating?

A clear protective coating that makes future graffiti easier to remove without damaging the surface underneath.

Stone Restoration

Can you clean limestone and marble safely?

Yes. Soft, acid-sensitive stones like limestone and marble are cleaned with pH-appropriate products and low pressure — never harsh acids.

Should natural stone be sealed?

Often, yes — especially patios and pool coping. Sealing helps resist staining. We advise based on the stone type and use.

Do you clean monuments and statues?

Yes. Monuments, memorials and statues are cleaned with gentle, conservation-appropriate methods.

Metal Restoration

Can you remove rust without damaging the metal?

Yes. We select the blasting media and pressure to suit the metal so the substrate is cleaned without unnecessary profile or thinning.

Do you restore architectural copper and aluminium?

Yes — including heritage copper and aluminium elements on historic buildings.
