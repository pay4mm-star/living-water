Living Water has an extensive track record fixing new and old efflorescence problems. We assess the material first and choose the right cleaning method rather than treating every surface the same — especially important for older brick, stone and heritage masonry, where aggressive cleaning causes damage.

Will efflorescence come back?

It can return if moisture keeps moving through the material. New masonry often blooms for a season while it cures. We explain the cause on your property and the right approach — including when sealing helps.

Questions

Efflorescence Removal FAQ

Anything not covered here? Call (416) 663-8000 and ask.

What causes efflorescence?

Water dissolves salts inside brick, mortar, concrete or stone, carries them to the surface and evaporates, leaving a white crystalline deposit.

Can pressure washing alone remove efflorescence?

Light, fresh efflorescence sometimes brushes or rinses off, but established deposits usually need a suitable masonry cleaner followed by controlled rinsing.

Is efflorescence covered by my stone warranty?

Many Ontario stone and paver manufacturers use Living Water as their warranty service provider. Check with your supplier or contact us.

How do you remove efflorescence from pavers and stone?

We use cleaners matched to the surface, applied and agitated carefully and rinsed thoroughly, because pressure washing alone only drives moisture back in. We then recommend letting the surface dry before sealing.

Will efflorescence come back?

It can return while moisture is still moving through the masonry, especially on new work. Removing it and then fixing moisture sources, and sealing when the surface is dry, reduces repeat blooms.

How much does efflorescence removal cost?

It depends on the material, the area and how severe the blooming is. Every job is a fixed written quote after a free assessment, so you know the price before work starts.
