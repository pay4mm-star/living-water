How to Hire a Pressure Washing Contractor in the GTA: 10 Questions to Ask

By the Living Water team · 28 years cleaning and restoring GTA properties · Updated November 2026

Short answer: Before hiring a pressure washing contractor in Ontario, confirm they carry commercial general liability insurance and WSIB coverage (and can send certificates), ask how they will clean each surface and protect plants, windows and runoff, get a fixed written quote with scope and timing, and look for verifiable reviews and before-and-after photos of similar work.

10 questions to ask

  1. Are you insured? Ask for a Certificate of Insurance showing commercial general liability. Living Water carries $5M.
  2. Are your workers covered by WSIB? In Ontario, hiring a contractor without WSIB coverage can expose the property owner to liability. Ask for a clearance certificate.
  3. How will you clean each surface? A professional distinguishes soft washing, hot-water pressure washing and blasting.
  4. Will you test first? Essential for brick, stone, heritage and paint removal.
  5. How will you protect plants, windows, vehicles and neighbours?
  6. How do you manage wash water and runoff? Municipal sewer bylaws restrict what can enter storm drains.
  7. Is the quote fixed and in writing? It should state method, scope, timing and price.
  8. Do you have photos of similar jobs?
  9. What happens if I'm not satisfied? Look for a re-clean guarantee.
  10. Are your crews certified for lifts and work at height? Needed for multi-storey facades.

Red flags

  • Cash-only, no written quote
  • No insurance certificate or WSIB clearance
  • "One pressure for everything"
  • Pressure to decide on the spot
