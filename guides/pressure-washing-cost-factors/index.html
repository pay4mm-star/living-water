Pressure Washing Cost in Toronto: What Affects the Price

By the Living Water team · 28 years cleaning and restoring GTA properties · Updated November 2026

  • Since 199828 years in business
  • 7,500+properties cleaned
  • $5Mliability insured
  • WSIBcovered & ISSA-GBAC
  • 4.9 / 5rated on Google

Short answer: Pressure washing prices in the GTA depend mainly on the area and type of surface, how heavy the staining or growth is, the cleaning method required (soft wash, hot water, chemical or blasting), access and height (ladders, lifts, containment), extras like re-sanding or sealing, and scheduling such as after-hours or emergency work. A site-specific, fixed written quote is the only reliable number.

On this page
  1. The main cost factors
  2. How to get an accurate quote
  3. Why the cheapest quote can cost more

The main cost factors

FactorWhy it matters
Size of the areaMore square footage means more time, water and product.
Surface typeDelicate surfaces (heritage brick, limestone, cedar) need slower, gentler methods.
Level of stainingOil, rust, paint, efflorescence and deep moss need pre-treatment or multiple passes.
MethodSoft washing, hot-water washing, chemical cleaning and media blasting use different equipment and materials.
Access & heightLadders, boom or scissor lifts, scaffolding and containment add set-up time.
Protection & runoffCovering plants, windows and vehicles, and managing wash water.
ExtrasPolymeric sand, sealing, anti-graffiti coatings, repairs.
SchedulingOvernight, weekend or 24/7 emergency work.

How to get an accurate quote

  • Send photos of each surface and any problem areas.
  • Give approximate dimensions and your postal code.
  • Mention access limits — tight side yards, heights, parking.
  • Ask for a fixed written quote with method and scope, not an hourly estimate.

Why the cheapest quote can cost more

Damage from the wrong method — etched concrete, washed-out mortar, stripped shingles, burned brick — is expensive or impossible to reverse. Compare insurance, WSIB coverage and method, not just price.
