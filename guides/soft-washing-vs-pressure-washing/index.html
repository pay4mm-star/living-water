Soft Washing vs Pressure Washing: Which Does Your Property Need?

By the Living Water team · 28 years cleaning and restoring GTA properties · Updated November 2026

  • Since 199828 years in business
  • 7,500+properties cleaned
  • $5Mliability insured
  • WSIBcovered & ISSA-GBAC
  • 4.9 / 5rated on Google

Short answer: Soft washing uses low pressure (similar to a garden hose) plus biodegradable cleaners to kill and dissolve algae, mould and mildew; pressure washing uses high-pressure water — often heated — to physically blast off dirt, oil and stains. Use soft washing on siding, stucco, roofs, painted wood and soft or heritage masonry; use pressure washing on concrete, interlock and other hard, durable surfaces.

What is soft washing?

Soft washing applies a cleaning solution at low pressure, lets it dwell so it can break down organic growth, then rinses gently. Because the chemistry does the work, it reaches algae and mould spores that pressure alone only knocks off the surface — so results last longer.

What is pressure washing?

Pressure washing relies on mechanical force. Professional units deliver high pressure and high flow, and hot-water machines (often called "power washing") cut through oil, grease, gum and tire marks far faster than cold water.

Which method for which surface?

SurfaceRecommended methodWhy
Vinyl and aluminium sidingSoft washHigh pressure forces water behind panels and can crack or dent them.
Stucco and EIFSSoft washPressure can gouge the finish and drive water into the wall.
Asphalt shingle, cedar or ceramic roofsSoft washPressure strips granules, splits shakes and can void warranties.
Wood decks and fencesLow pressure / soft washHigh pressure raises and furrows the grain.
Heritage or soft brick and stoneSoft wash, steam or gentle media blastingPressure erodes the brick face and washes out mortar.
Concrete driveways and garage floorsHot-water pressure washDurable surface; heat lifts oil and grease.
Interlock and paversPressure wash with surface cleaner, then re-sandRemoves moss and staining; joints are re-filled afterwards.
Commercial sidewalks and plazasHot-water pressure washRemoves gum, grease and heavy foot-traffic grime.

What happens when the wrong method is used?

  • Etched "wand marks" and stripes in concrete
  • Sand blown out of interlock joints, leading to weeds and shifting
  • Mortar washed out of brick joints, letting water in
  • Water forced behind siding and into window seals
  • Shingle granules stripped, shortening roof life

Most homes need both

A typical GTA home combines a soft-washed house, roof and deck with a pressure-washed driveway and walkway. A professional crew will tell you which surface gets which method before work starts.

Is soft washing as effective as pressure washing?

For organic growth like algae, mould and mildew it is usually more effective, because the cleaner kills the growth instead of just knocking off the surface layer.

Is soft washing safe for plants?

Professional crews pre-wet and rinse plants and use biodegradable cleaners to protect landscaping.

What is the difference between power washing and pressure washing?

The terms are often used interchangeably; 'power washing' usually refers to pressure washing with heated water.
